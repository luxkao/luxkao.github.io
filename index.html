About

I am a software developer with an interest in creative programming, complex systems, and computational research.

My interests lie in building and exploring systems through code, whether as a creative medium, a research instrument, or a way of understanding complex phenomena.

My previous academic work at the Instituto Federal de Pernambuco (IFPE) involved developing computational tools for environmental research, supported by a scientific initiation scholarship. More recently, I have become interested in the behaviour and interactions of autonomous AI agents.

Publications

Previous research at IFPE focused on developing computational tools for urban environmental assessment. The work moved from measurement to tooling to documentation: studies of how the green area index is defined and calculated, a low-code web application that computes it without GIS expertise, and a guide for the people who use that application.

English titles and summaries are provided for accessibility; original Portuguese titles and source documents are linked. Summaries are editorial notes written for this page, not the authors' abstracts.

Development of a low-code application for calculating the green area index in urban environments

Desenvolvimento de aplicação low code para o cálculo do índice de área verde em contexto urbano official English title

Gerlany Lacerda Dias, Lucas de Melo Falcão, Vânia Soares de Carvalho, Ioná Maria Beltrão Rameh Barbosa, Christianne Torres de Paiva, Diogo Henrique Fernandes da Paz

Journal article · Revista Derecho y Cambio Social 22 (85), article e3984, pp. 1–18, 2025

DOI 10.54899/dcs.v22i85.3984 · Article page · Full text (PDF)

Summary The full-length version of the low-code calculator first presented at CONNEPI in 2024. The authors set out functional and non-functional requirements, assemble LiDAR-derived mosaics from the Pernambuco Tridimensional (PE3D) programme, and implement what ArcGIS Experience Builder does not provide — storing delimited polygons and summing their areas — as custom TypeScript and React widgets against the ESRI API. The application computes the green area index for municipalities of the Metropolitan Region of Recife and reports it alongside total green area and resident population. The paper argues that the low-code route shortened development and produced an interface usable by people without GIS training, while noting that non-native features still required conventional programming and that thin platform documentation remains a barrier.

The journal publishes a Portuguese, an English and a Spanish abstract. Read the official English abstract on the article page.

The importance of nature conservation units in calculating the green area index: reflections on the environmental indicator in Administrative Region 04 of the municipality of Recife, Pernambuco

A importância das unidades de conservação da natureza no cálculo do índice de área verde: reflexões sobre o indicador ambiental na Região Político-Administrativa 04 do município de Recife-PE translated title

Gerlany Lacerda Dias, Lucas de Melo Falcão, Vânia Soares de Carvalho, Ioná Maria Beltrão Rameh Barbosa, Aida Araújo Ferreira

Conference extended abstract · Anais do XIV Congresso Norte-Nordeste de Pesquisa e Inovação (CONNEPI), 2024

Conference record · Full text (PDF)

Award Best work in the Applied Social Sciences area, poster session (Melhor trabalho na área de Ciências Sociais Aplicadas na Mostra de Banner), XIV CONNEPI, 2024. IFPE announcement · IFPA report

Summary A study of one administrative region of Recife: RPA-04, twelve neighbourhoods on the western side of the city, chosen because it holds the largest share of forest-type conservation units. The index is computed twice for each neighbourhood — counting conservation-unit areas as public green space, and excluding them — against a population estimate projected from census data, because 2022 neighbourhood counts were not yet available. The difference is large: in Várzea the index falls from about 157 m² per inhabitant to 0.35, in Caxangá from 84 to 0.18; across the seven neighbourhoods that contain conservation units it drops by between 12% and 99%, and all of them end below the 15 m² per inhabitant reference used in the Brazilian literature. The argument is that the indicator's meaning depends on a definitional choice that is rarely made explicit, and that conservation units carry the region's environmental quality while being unevenly distributed.

Development of a low-code application for sizing green areas in an urban environment

Desenvolvimento de aplicação low code para dimensionamento de áreas verdes em ambiente urbano translated title

Lucas de Melo Falcão, Gerlany Lacerda Dias, Vânia Soares de Carvalho, Ioná Maria Beltrão Rameh Barbosa, Aida Araújo Ferreira

Conference extended abstract · Anais do XIV Congresso Norte-Nordeste de Pesquisa e Inovação (CONNEPI), 2024

Conference record · Full text (PDF)

Summary The first report of the low-code calculator, written as the software-side counterpart to the conservation-unit study. It states the problem — the Metropolitan Region of Recife has no consolidated green area index, and computing one demands GIS skills municipal teams may not have — and then describes the build: six functional requirements and one non-functional requirement elicited with the project team, a cartographic base from PE3D at 1:1000 scale, 2022 census population figures, and an application assembled in ArcGIS Experience Builder with custom TypeScript and React widgets for what the platform lacks. Green areas are delimited as polygons, summed, and divided by population in the standard formulation; street and isolated trees are excluded as vegetation cover rather than public green space. The authors report fast development and easy customisation on the low-code platform, and limited ESRI API documentation as a real obstacle for users without programming experience.

Guide for using web tools to analyse and calculate the green area index in the city of Recife, Pernambuco

Guia para utilização de ferramentas web para a análise e cálculo do índice de área verde na cidade do Recife-PE translated title

Gerlany Lacerda Dias, Vânia Soares de Carvalho, Ioná Maria Beltrão Rameh Barbosa, Lucas de Melo Falcão, Aida Araújo Ferreira

Technical guide, 14 pp., in Portuguese · IFPE Institutional Repository, 2024 · CC BY 4.0

Repository record · Full text (PDF)

Summary A user manual for the two applications produced by the research programme: a dashboard for managing green areas, and a web system that calculates square metres of green space per inhabitant for a chosen region. It walks through the interface element by element — theme, layout, header, list, map, indicator, embedded content — and then through the calculation workflow, closing with brief final considerations. It is addressed to environmental managers in public bodies and private companies who need to verify data for planning, decision-making and monitoring, and it notes that both tools are meant for a notebook or desktop rather than a smartphone. The repository record credits development to Gerlany Lacerda Dias within the IFPE professional master's programme in environmental management, with the support of undergraduate student Lucas de Melo Falcão.

Also relevant to this work: the master's dissertation behind the conservation-unit study, Instrumentos de gestão de áreas verdes para implementação de políticas públicas de qualidade ambiental para a cidade do Recife – PE (Gerlany Lacerda Dias, IFPE, 2024), which acknowledges the author's participation as a scientific initiation student.

Interests

Creative programming
I am particularly interested in programming as a creative medium, especially through game development, procedural generation, interactive systems, and computational simulations. I enjoy exploring how simple rules and interactions can give rise to complex and unexpected outcomes.
Autonomous agents
The behaviour, cooperation, and adaptation of autonomous AI agents, particularly in persistent environments where interactions accumulate over time.
Complex systems
Emergence, self-organisation, and the relationship between local interactions and system-level behaviour. I am interested in both studying these phenomena and constructing computational environments in which they can be explored.
